Privacy Policy — Partner Match

This policy covers the Partner Match web widget only.

1. Birth Information You Enter

All calculations in this Widget run entirely in your own browser. The birth date, birth time, and UTC offset entered for each of the two people ("Him" and "Her") are used only to compute the match reading on your device for the moment you use the Widget. This information is not sent to, or stored on, any server operated by the developer.

2. No Location Data Is Used or Collected

Unlike some other widgets in this family, Partner Match does not ask for or use any birth place, current location, or device location. The underlying calculation (Sun, Moon, and Venus positions) depends only on birth date, time, and UTC offset, so no geocoding service and no location permission of any kind is involved.

3. Third-Party Services

The Widget does not call any third-party API or service to perform its calculations — everything runs locally in your browser using built-in astronomical formulas. The only third-party resource loaded is the Google Fonts stylesheet used for the Widget's typography, which is subject to Google's own privacy practices.

4. This Website

This widget is designed to be embedded on lifeprediction.net, which is currently hosted using GoDaddy and built on the Nicepage platform. Like most website hosting arrangements, this may involve the automatic collection of standard technical data as a normal part of serving web pages — for example, IP address, browser type, and access times — which is handled by the hosting provider rather than actively collected by the developer. The hosting provider and platform used for this website may change over time. The widget's own code is additionally hosted on GitHub Pages.

5. Analytics

This website may use one or more third-party analytics services to measure visits and engagement. These services may collect standard technical information such as IP address, device/browser type, approximate location, and pages viewed. The specific analytics provider(s) in use may change over time; current details can be requested via the contact method below.

6. Data About Other People

If you enter another person's birth details into the "Him" or "Her" fields, that data is processed the same way as your own — locally in your browser only, and not transmitted to or stored by the developer. You are responsible for having an appropriate basis to enter someone else's information (see the Disclaimer for more on this).

7. What This Policy Does Not Cover

This policy covers this web widget and the lifeprediction.net website only. It does not cover any third-party service you may be directed to (for example, Google Fonts or GitHub), each of which has its own separate privacy practices.

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time, including to reflect changes in hosting provider, analytics provider, or the services this widget relies on. Continued use of the widget or website after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
